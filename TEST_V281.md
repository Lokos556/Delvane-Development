# Rosa V2.8.1 quick test

1. Start `START_ROSA.bat` and log in as owner.
2. Open Settings. Confirm there is no Guest Profiles or Registration Center row.
3. Open Today. If an arrival today/tomorrow has incomplete registration, a Registration needs attention strip appears. Click the guest and confirm the stay workspace opens.
4. Open an existing reservation. Confirm a Guest context card appears for a linked guest. Returning guests show stay count and preferences. `View guest history` opens the full historical profile.
5. In Reservation → Preparation, confirm Guest registration shows a progress bar and individual guest records. If incomplete, click `Prepare reminder`. Then `Open Reception`; the draft should exist under Scheduled.
6. Open Reception on a linked returning guest. Confirm the guest-context strip appears above the conversation.
7. Press Ctrl+K and search a guest name. Guest search results should show stay count; clicking the result opens guest history.
8. Confirm Rosa AI still answers registration and guest-memory questions.
