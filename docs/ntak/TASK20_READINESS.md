## EU_02B historical stock correction

**Root cause.** `physical_units.capacity` projected physical rooms at the business date, but compared that total to the current `room_types.inventory_count`. Deactivating the extra unit for TX+1 reduced the physical catalog from five rooms to four and left the old comparison count in place. The daily close correctly blocked on the mismatch. The existing room-type API creates selling stock but cannot record its historical count. Reservation changes and cancellations move demand across dates or release allocations. The Channex ARI override caps date-specific channel availability after accounting for booked demand. Neither operation records the number of physical rooms in service inventory on a prior date.

**Operator workflow.** In the property's **Apartments and rooms** view, select the prior business date and refresh. Correct the effective physical room record, then choose **Correct historical room count** for the mismatching room type. Check the count and enter the reason. Return to daily closing and create the corrected revision with `TECH_HIBA_ADATJAVITAS` for this case. The Hungarian labels are available in the same view.

Migration **3090** adds `historical_room_stock` in `physical_units.py`, registered by `app.py`. The owner-only `PUT /api/properties/{property_id}/physical-units/historical-stock/{room_type_id}` records the reason, actor, date and version. The count must match the active physical catalog for that room type and date. OOO/OOS units remain part of the catalog, and planned allocations and channel overrides are separate. The property lock, CSRF, role and tenant checks apply. The latest recorded count is compared again during close, so any later mismatch still blocks. Only the selected closed date is marked for review by the stock action. Previous count revisions and encrypted closing snapshots are retained. Selling stock, reservation capacity, overrides and provider queue handling continue to use their existing sources.

The case test in `tests/test_ntak_task20_rehearsal.py` records and corrects both dated counts through the API, with the stale-count rejection exercised between them. `tests/test_physical_units.py` covers invalid counts and dates, cross-property and role denial, CSRF, retries, date isolation and persistence. `tests/test_flutter_distribution.py` checks the live selling stock and override across booking creation, modification and cancellation with a mocked Channex connection. `inventory_controls_test.dart` checks the Flutter action, submitted date/count and owner/manager permissions.
